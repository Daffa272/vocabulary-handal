Step 2 · Learn the chunks
What do you do + [time/situation] ?

Ask what someone normally does in a particular time or situation.

What do you do on weekends? What do you do after work? What do you do when you're bored?
In my free time, I usually + [V1] .

A useful pattern for talking about your routine.

In my free time, I usually read books. In my free time, I usually play games. In my free time, I usually watch movies.
I enjoy + [V-ing] .

Use this pattern to express activities you enjoy.

I enjoy reading books. I enjoy playing games. I enjoy watching movies.
