4–10. Profitability: ROA, ROE, NIM, CASA, Cost of Funds

📐 ROA — Return on Assets

Seberapa efektif bank menghasilkan laba dari aset yang dimilikinya?
ROA = Net Income ÷ Average Total Assets
Net Income Rp2 T, Average Assets Rp100 T → ROA = 2/100 = 2%
Artinya: setiap Rp100 aset menghasilkan ±Rp2 laba.
Kenapa pakai Average Assets?

Aset bank berubah sepanjang tahun. Awal tahun Rp90 T, akhir tahun Rp110 T → rata-rata = (90+110)/2 = Rp100 T. Lebih representatif daripada aset akhir tahun saja.

📐 ROE — Return on Equity

Seberapa besar return yang dihasilkan terhadap modal pemegang saham?
ROE = Net Income ÷ Average Equity
Net Income Rp2 T, Average Equity Rp20 T → ROE = 2/20 = 10%

⚖️ ROA vs ROE — peran leverage

Assets Rp100 T = Liabilities 90 T + Equity 10 T. Net Income Rp2 T:

ROA = 2/100 = 2%  •  ROE = 2/10 = 20%

ROE jauh lebih tinggi karena bank pakai leverage: Rp10 T equity mendukung Rp100 T assets.

⚠️ ROE tinggi ≠ otomatis sehat. ROE bisa naik karena laba naik, equity relatif kecil, atau leverage naik. ROE harus dibaca bersama CAR dan risiko.

📐 NIM — Net Interest Margin

Seberapa besar margin bunga bersih dari earning assets?
NIM = Net Interest Income ÷ Average Earning Assets
NII = Interest Income − Interest Expense
Interest Income Rp8 T − Interest Expense Rp3 T = NII Rp5 T
NII 5 / Avg EA 100 = NIM 5%

Faktor yang mempengaruhi NIM: loan yield, cost of funds, komposisi earning assets (loans, securities, placements), komposisi funding (giro, tabungan, deposito, wholesale).

💰 CASA — Current Account + Savings Account (Giro + Tabungan)

CASA Ratio = (Giro + Tabungan) ÷ Total Deposits
Deposits Rp100 T, Giro Rp30 T, Tabungan Rp40 T → CASA = 70/100 = 70%
FundingDanaCost
GiroRp30 T1%
TabunganRp40 T2%
DepositoRp30 T5%

Proporsi dana murah tinggi → cost of funds rendah → NIM lebih terjaga. Tapi hubungannya tidak otomatis — tetap dipengaruhi pricing kredit, suku bunga pasar, dan struktur aset.

💸 Cost of Funds

CoF = Interest Expense ÷ Average Interest-Bearing Liabilities
Interest expense Rp4 T / Rp100 T = 4%

Funding makin mahal → tekanan ke margin bunga jika yield aset tidak ikut naik.

11–16. Asset Quality: NPL, Credit Cost, Coverage

📉 NPL — Non-Performing Loan

NPL = Non-Performing Loans ÷ Total Loans
NPL Rp2 T / Loans Rp100 T = 2%
NPL ↑→ Credit Loss ↑→ Provision/CKPN ↑→ Profit ↓→ Capital ↓
Gross NPL vs Net NPL

Gross NPL: kredit bermasalah sebelum cadangan. Net NPL: setelah memperhitungkan coverage/CKPN. Selalu cek definisi di laporan bank/OJK.

📉 Credit Cost

Credit Cost = Credit Loss Expense ÷ Average Loans
Credit loss Rp500 M / Avg loans Rp100 T = 0,5%

🎯 NPL ≠ Credit Cost

Bank A: NPL 1%, Credit Cost 0,3%. Bank B: NPL 1%, Credit Cost 1%. NPL sama, credit cost beda!

Penyebabnya: probability of default, severity of loss, collateral, recovery, provisioning, perubahan kualitas kredit, komposisi portofolio.

🛡️ Loan Loss Coverage

Coverage = CKPN (Loan Loss Allowance) ÷ NPL
NPL Rp2 T, CKPN Rp2,5 T → Coverage = 2,5/2 = 125%

Catatan: definisi coverage tiap bank bisa beda — jangan campur formula antarbank tanpa cek definisi.

17–19. Efficiency: BOPO & CIR

⚙️ BOPO

BOPO = Operating Expenses ÷ Operating Income × 100%
Opex Rp4 T / Op. Income Rp5 T = 80%
Setiap Rp100 pendapatan operasional, biaya operasional ±Rp80.

BOPO ↓ → efisiensi membaik. BOPO ↑ → biaya relatif terhadap pendapatan naik. Tapi jangan menyimpulkan dari satu periode — bisa dipengaruhi income naik/turun, restructuring, investasi teknologi, ekspansi cabang, atau credit cost (tergantung definisi).

⚙️ CIR — Cost to Income Ratio

CIR = Operating Costs ÷ Operating Income

Konsepnya mirip BOPO, tapi definisi komponen bisa berbeda antar bank/laporan/analis. Jangan anggap BOPO dan CIR selalu identik.

20–24. Liquidity: LDR, LCR, NSFR

Bank bisa profitable tapi kena liquidity problem — karena pola short-term funding → long-term lending.

💧 LDR — Loan to Deposit Ratio

LDR = Total Loans ÷ Total Deposits × 100%
Loans Rp80 T / Deposits Rp100 T = 80%
LDR Rendah
Konservatif, liquidity buffer besar, tapi asset utilization rendah
LDR Tinggi
Agresif menyalurkan kredit, earning assets besar, tapi tekanan likuiditas jika funding tidak stabil

LDR tinggi tidak otomatis bagus/buruk — lihat bersama NIM, NPL, CASA, liquidity buffer, funding stability, capital.

💧 LCR — Liquidity Coverage Ratio

LCR = HQLA ÷ Net Cash Outflows (30 hari)
HQLA Rp120 T / Outflow Rp100 T = 120%

Tujuan: kemampuan bank bertahan menghadapi tekanan likuiditas jangka pendek (short-term resilience).

💧 NSFR — Net Stable Funding Ratio

NSFR = Available Stable Funding ÷ Required Stable Funding

Fokusnya lebih panjang: stabilitas struktur pendanaan.

LCR → tahan liquidity stress jangka pendek  |  NSFR → struktur pendanaan stabil horizon lebih panjang.

25–30. Capital: CAR, Buffer, CET1, dan hubungan dengan ROE

🏛️ CAR — Capital Adequacy Ratio

CAR = Capital ÷ Risk Weighted Assets (ATMR)
Capital Rp15 T / RWA Rp100 T = 15%

Kenapa RWA, bukan Total Assets?

AssetNilaiRisiko
CashRp10 TRelatif rendah
Government securitiesRp20 TTergantung karakteristik/regulasi
Corporate loansRp30 TLebih tinggi
Risky loansRp10 TLebih tinggi

Tidak semua aset berisiko sama → regulator pakai Risk Weighted Assets (RWA).

Capital sebagai buffer

Assets 100 T = Liab 90 T + Equity 10 T
Loss Rp3 T → Equity = 10 − 3 = Rp7 T

Modal = buffer penyerap kerugian. Makin besar risiko yang ditanggung, makin penting kemampuan modal menyerap kerugian.

CET1, Tier 1, Total Capital

  • CET1 (Common Equity Tier 1): modal kualitas tertinggi untuk menyerap kerugian.
  • Tier 1: modal inti.
  • Total Capital: mencakup komponen modal yang memenuhi persyaratan permodalan regulasi.
⚠️ ROE & CAR: leverage naik → assets naik relatif ke equity → ROE bisa naik, tapi sensitivitas terhadap kerugian naik dan capital buffer tertekan. Selalu baca ROE bersama CAR.

35–37. Hubungan Antar-Rasio: 3 Skenario

📉 Skenario 1 — Economic Slowdown

Pendapatan debitur ↓→ NPL ↑→ ECL ↑→ Credit Cost ↑→ ROA ↓→ ROE ↓→ CAR ↓

📈 Skenario 2 — Suku Bunga Naik

Loan yield 8% − CoF 4% = spread 4%
CoF naik ke 6% → spread 8% − 6% = 2% → NIM ↓

Tanpa penyesuaian pricing atau asset mix, profitability ikut tertekan.

🚀 Skenario 3 — Credit Growth 20%

Rp100 T × 1,20 = Rp120 T

Growth tinggi, tapi harus ditanya: apakah kualitas tetap baik? Jika loan growth ↑ tapi NPL ↑ dan credit cost ↑ → pertumbuhan perlu dianalisis lebih lanjut. Loan growth ≠ quality growth.
