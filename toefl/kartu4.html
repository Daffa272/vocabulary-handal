Ringkasan Materi Penting + 25 Latihan Soal

Subject–Verb Agreement • Passive • Clause • Parallelism • Gerund • Infinitive

📚 A. MATERI TOEFL STRUCTURE

1. Subject–Verb Agreement

Keselarasan Subjek & Verb

Verb harus sesuai dengan jumlah subject.

Singular Subject → V1 + s/es
Plural Subject → V1
Contoh:
✓ The student works hard.
✓ The students work hard.
✓ Each student has a book.
Ingat: kata each, every, everyone, everybody, someone, nobody dianggap singular.

2. Passive Voice

Kalimat Pasif

Passive voice menekankan tindakan atau objek yang menerima tindakan.

Subject + BE + V3
The report was written by the manager.
The buildings are constructed every year.
Perhatikan tense pada auxiliary be: is, am, are, was, were, has been, dll.

3. Reduced Relative Clause

Klausa Relatif Disederhanakan

Relative clause dapat dipersingkat jika strukturnya memungkinkan.

Active → Noun + V-ing
Passive → Noun + V3
The man standing near the door is my teacher.
The book written by Shakespeare is famous.

Standing = active.
Written = passive.

4. Infinitive (to + Verb)

Infinitive sering digunakan untuk menyatakan tujuan, rencana, atau alasan.

to + V1
I want to study English.
She went to the library to read a book.
They need to improve their skills.
Setelah "to" sebagai infinitive, gunakan V1, bukan V-ing atau V2.

5. Subjunctive / Bare Infinitive

Digunakan setelah kata tertentu seperti recommend, suggest, insist, demand, dan request.

Subject + recommend/suggest/insist + that + Subject + V1
The teacher suggested that he study harder.
The manager insisted that everyone be on time.
Dalam pola TOEFL ini, setelah that + subject digunakan bentuk dasar verb.

6. So...that / Such...that / Too...to

so + adjective + that + clause
such + noun + that + clause
too + adjective + to + V1
The test was so difficult that many students failed.
It was such a difficult test that many students failed.
The box is too heavy to lift.

7. Comparison

Digunakan untuk membandingkan dua atau lebih hal.

Comparative → -er / more
Superlative → -est / most
John is taller than Mark.
This is the most interesting book.
The more you practice, the better you become.

8. Inversion

Inversion adalah pembalikan posisi auxiliary dan subject.

Negative expression + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb
Never have I seen such a beautiful place.
Rarely does he visit his hometown.
Not only is she intelligent, but she is also kind.
Kata seperti never, rarely, hardly, seldom, not only dapat memicu inversion.

9. Noun Clause

Noun clause berfungsi sebagai noun: dapat menjadi subject, object, atau complement.

that / whether / if / what / who / how + clause
I know that he is honest.
She doesn't know where he lives.
The question is how we can solve it.
Setelah kata tanya dalam noun clause, jangan menggunakan pola pertanyaan langsung.
✓ I know where he lives.
✗ I know where does he live.

10. Adjective Clause

Adjective clause memberikan informasi tambahan mengenai noun.

Noun + who / whom / whose / which / that + clause
The woman who lives next door is my aunt.
The book that I bought yesterday is interesting.

11. Gerund vs Infinitive

Gerund = V-ing yang berfungsi sebagai noun.

Gerund → V-ing
Infinitive → to + V1
I enjoy reading books.
She wants to study abroad.
He decided to leave early.
Beberapa verb harus diikuti gerund, sedangkan yang lain diikuti infinitive.

12. Parallel Structure

Elemen yang sejajar harus menggunakan bentuk grammar yang sama.

V-ing + V-ing + V-ing
to V1 + to V1 + to V1
✓ She likes reading, swimming, and traveling.
✗ She likes reading, to swim, and traveling.

Kata seperti and, or, but, not only...but also sering menjadi petunjuk parallelism.

13. Participle: -ing vs -ed

V-ing → aktif / melakukan
V3 / -ed → pasif / dikenai
The girl smiling over there is my sister.
The letter written yesterday was important.
Tanyakan: noun tersebut melakukan tindakan atau menerima tindakan?

14. Preposition & Conjunction

Preposition diikuti noun/pronoun/gerund.

Preposition + noun / pronoun / V-ing
He is interested in learning English.
She apologized for being late.
They talked about studying abroad.
Setelah preposition, gunakan V-ing, bukan V1.

15. Number Expression

Perhatikan penggunaan angka dan noun singular/plural.

Number + plural countable noun
Percentage + of + noun
✓ 70 percent of the population
✓ 70 percent of the students
✓ three books
✗ three book

🎯 B. TRIK CEPAT MENGERJAKAN TOEFL STRUCTURE

STEP 1 — Cari Subject
Siapa yang melakukan atau mengalami tindakan?

STEP 2 — Cari Verb utama
Pastikan kalimat mempunyai verb yang tepat.

STEP 3 — Periksa pola setelah kata kunci
Preposition → V-ing
want/need/decide → to + V1
make/let/have → object + V1
recommend/suggest → that + subject + V1

STEP 4 — Perhatikan singular/plural
Each/every/everyone → singular.

STEP 5 — Perhatikan parallel structure
and/or/but → bentuk yang sejajar.
