Subject-Verb Agreement: Materi Lengkap

🎯 Tujuan Utama

Saat mengerjakan soal seperti ini, jangan langsung menerjemahkan kalimat. Gunakan urutan:

1. Cari subject → 2. Tentukan singular/plural → 3. Cari tense → 4. Pilih verb

Contoh:

The quality of the products ___ excellent.

Jangan terkecoh oleh products.

  • Subject = The quality
  • of the products = hanya keterangan
  • quality = singular
  • Maka → is ✅

1️⃣ Subject Singular → Singular Verb

Subject singular menggunakan verb singular.

Simple Present

SubjectVerb
Iwork
Youwork
Wework
Theywork
Heworks
Sheworks
Itworks

Untuk he/she/it, biasanya verb mendapat -s/-es.

Contoh:

The student works hard.

The manager arrives early.

❌ Jebakan TOEFL:

The quality of the products is excellent. ✅

~~The quality of the products are excellent.~~ ❌

2️⃣ Subject Plural → Plural Verb

Subject plural menggunakan plural verb.

The students work hard.

The effects of pollution are serious.

Subject = effects → plural.

3️⃣ Jangan Terkecoh oleh of + noun

Ini salah satu jebakan paling sering muncul.

Pola: Subject + of + noun + verb

Contoh: The list of participants is available.

Subject = list → singular.

The effects of pollution are dangerous.

Subject = effects → plural.

🚨 Rumus cepat: The X of Y + verb
Lihat X, bukan Y.

4️⃣ A Number of vs The Number of

Ini sangat penting untuk TOEFL.

A number of + plural noun → PLURAL VERB

A number of students are absent.

A number of researchers have participated.

The number of + plural noun → SINGULAR VERB

The number of students is increasing.

The number of applicants has increased.

🚀 Trik super cepat:
A number → ARE
The number → IS

5️⃣ Indefinite Pronouns: Everyone, Everybody, Each

Kata-kata berikut dianggap singular:

  • everyone, everybody, someone, somebody
  • anyone, anybody, no one, nobody
  • each, every

Everyone is ready.

Nobody knows the answer.

Each student has a book.

6️⃣ Each of + Plural Noun → Singular Verb

Each of the students has a computer.

Each of the participants is required to register.

Cara cepat: Each of the students has... ✅

7️⃣ Each A and Each B → Singular Verb

Each student and each teacher is required to register.

8️⃣ And Biasanya → Plural

John and Mary are students.

The manager and the employees have agreed.

🚨 Tetapi hati-hati:

Bread and butter is my favorite breakfast.

9️⃣ Either...or dan Neither...nor

Verb mengikuti subject yang paling dekat dengan verb.

Either the teacher or the students are responsible.

Neither the manager nor the employees are available.

Neither the employees nor the manager is available.

🚀 Trik TOEFL:
Lihat B! (subject terdekat dengan verb)

🔟 Together with, Along with, As well as

Kata-kata ini tidak membuat subject utama menjadi plural.

The manager, together with several employees, is attending.

The president, along with his advisors, is attending.

1️⃣1️⃣ Relative Clause: Who / Which / That

Plural: The students who participate in the program are required.

Singular: The student who participates in the program is required.

1️⃣2️⃣ One of + Plural Noun

Pola: One of + plural noun + singular verb

One of the students is absent.

One of the researchers has resigned.

1️⃣3️⃣ One of the + plural noun + who...

She is one of the researchers who are responsible.

She is the only one of the researchers who is responsible.

1️⃣4️⃣ Percentage / Persentase

Pola: Percentage + of + noun + verb

Verb mengikuti noun setelah of.

Singular/uncountable noun

Fifty percent of the water is polluted.

Thirty percent of the population has access.

Plural noun

Fifty percent of the students are absent.

Thirty percent of the houses were damaged.

🚀 Rumus super cepat:
50% of the students → are
50% of the water → is

1️⃣5️⃣ The Percentage of

the percentage sendiri adalah subject singular.

The percentage of students is increasing.

Bandingkan:

Fifty percent of the students are absent. ✅

The percentage of students is increasing. ✅

1️⃣6️⃣ All of, Some of, None of

Verb mengikuti noun setelah of.

All of the information is useful.

All of the students are ready.

Some of the water is polluted.

Some of the students are absent.

None of the equipment is working.

None of the students are ready.

1️⃣9️⃣ Uncountable Nouns

Biasanya dianggap singular:

  • information, equipment, furniture
  • advice, research, knowledge
  • news, money

The information is useful.

The equipment is expensive.

2️⃣0️⃣ Piece(s) of Information

A piece of information is useful.

Several pieces of information are useful.

2️⃣1️⃣ There is / There are

There is a student in the room.

There are several students in the room.

🚀 Trik:
Setelah there, cari noun.
There ___ a book... → is
There ___ many books... → are

2️⃣2️⃣ Inversion

Pola: Place + verb + subject

On the table are several books.

Behind the building is a small laboratory.

Cara: Cari subject setelah verb.

2️⃣3️⃣ More Than One vs More Than Two

More than one student has complained. ✅

More than two students have complained. ✅

2️⃣4️⃣ Subject + Prepositional Phrase

The student in the classrooms is studying.

The students in the classroom are studying.

Pola: Subject + in/on/at/with/of + noun + verb

2️⃣7️⃣ The Cost of A and B

The cost of transportation and accommodation is included.

2️⃣8️⃣ Collective Nouns

Diperlakukan sebagai singular:

  • team, family, committee, government
  • audience, class, group

The team is ready.

The committee has reached a decision.

2️⃣9️⃣ Subject Dipisahkan oleh Koma

The manager, together with his employees, is attending.

John, as well as his friends, has arrived.

Frasa pengganggu: together with, along with, as well as, accompanied by, in addition to, including

⚡ STRATEGI 5 DETIK SAAT TOEFL

Contoh: The quality of the materials used in these buildings ___ much better.
Step 1 — Coret bagian pengganggu: The quality ~~of the materials...~~ ___
Step 2 — Tentukan subject: quality → Singular.
Step 3 — Pilih verb: is ✅

🎯 PETA MATERI

🟢 LEVEL 1

  • Singular vs plural
  • and
  • of + noun
  • percentage
  • all/some/none
  • there is/are

🟡 LEVEL 2

  • A number vs the number
  • each
  • either/neither
  • together with/along with
  • inversion
  • more than one
  • one of
  • relative clause

🔴 LEVEL 3

  • Subject + multiple modifiers
  • embedded clauses
  • complex inversion
  • reduced clauses
  • collective nouns
  • tricky relative clauses
  • multiple distractor nouns
