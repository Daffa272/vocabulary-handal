1 WILL + V1 — Simple Future

Digunakan untuk menyatakan sesuatu yang akan terjadi di masa depan.

Rumus

S + will + V1

Contoh

  • I will study tomorrow.
  • The company will open a new branch next year.
Ingat Setelah will, gunakan V1   ✅ will go   ❌ will went / will going

2 WILL BE + V-ING — Future Continuous

Digunakan untuk menyatakan aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa depan.

Rumus

S + will be + V-ing

Contoh

  • This time tomorrow, I will be traveling to Jakarta.
  • At 8 p.m. tonight, she will be studying.
Kata petunjuk this time tomorrow · this time next week · at this time next month · at 8 p.m. tomorrow

3 WILL HAVE + V3 — Future Perfect

Digunakan untuk menyatakan aktivitas yang sudah selesai sebelum waktu tertentu di masa depan.

Rumus

S + will have + V3

Contoh

  • By next month, I will have completed the course.
  • By 2030, scientists will have developed new technologies.
Kata petunjuk: by + waktu masa depan by tomorrow · by next week · by the end of this year · by 2030

4 WILL HAVE BEEN + V-ING — Future Perfect Continuous

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan aktivitas yang akan sudah berlangsung selama periode tertentu sampai suatu titik di masa depan.

Rumus

S + will have been + V-ing

Contoh

  • By next Friday, I will have been working here for six months.
  • By the time she retires, she will have been teaching for thirty years.
Kata petunjuk: for + durasi for three hours · for six months · for ten years · for thirty years

5 WOULD + V1

Would + V1 memiliki beberapa penggunaan penting dalam TOEFL.

A. Conditional

Digunakan untuk kondisi hipotetis.

If + S + V2, S + would + V1
  • If I had more money, I would travel abroad.
  • If she knew the answer, she would tell us.

6 SECOND CONDITIONAL ⭐

Digunakan untuk kondisi yang tidak nyata atau hipotetis pada saat sekarang.

Rumus

If + S + V2, S + would + V1

Contoh

  • If I had more free time, I would learn another language.
  • If he were here, he would help us.
Pola utama V2 → would + V1  |  If I knew → I would tell  |  If she had → she would buy

7 THIRD CONDITIONAL

Digunakan untuk kondisi masa lalu yang tidak terjadi.

Rumus

If + S + had + V3, S + would have + V3

Contoh

  • If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
  • If she had arrived earlier, she would have met him.
Pola utama had + V3 → would have + V3

8 First, Second, & Third Conditional

Ini adalah salah satu pola terpenting untuk TOEFL.

JenisIf ClauseMain Clause
FirstV1will + V1
SecondV2would + V1
Thirdhad + V3would have + V3

Contoh

  • First: If I have time, I will study.
  • Second: If I had time, I would study.
  • Third: If I had had time, I would have studied.
Shortcut V1 → WILL  |  V2 → WOULD  |  HAD + V3 → WOULD HAVE + V3

9 WOULD dalam Reported Speech

Would dapat digunakan ketika will diubah menjadi bentuk lampau dalam reported speech.

Direct SpeechHe said, "I will come."
Reported SpeechHe said that he would come.

Perubahan umum

10 Future in the Past

Digunakan ketika seseorang pada masa lalu membicarakan sesuatu yang akan terjadi setelah waktu tersebut.

WILL → WOULD
  • He said that he would return. — Dia mengatakan bahwa dia akan kembali.
  • I thought she would come.
  • They believed the company would succeed.

11 WAS/WERE GOING TO

Digunakan untuk menyatakan rencana atau sesuatu yang diperkirakan akan terjadi dari sudut pandang masa lalu.

Rumus

S + was/were going to + V1
  • I was going to study that night.
  • They were going to build a new office.

Perbandingan

Present/FutureI am going to study.
PastI was going to study.

12 WILL vs WOULD

kemauanpermintaan yang lebih sopan
  • I will help you. / She will arrive tomorrow.
  • I would help if I could. / He said he would come. / Would you like some coffee?

13 WOULD LIKE

Digunakan untuk menyampaikan keinginan secara sopan.

would like + noun

I would like some coffee.

would like + to + V1
  • I would like to study abroad.
  • Would you like to join us?
  • Would you like to help me?
⚠️ Setelah would like, jika diikuti verb gunakan to + V1

14 WILL / WOULD + BE + V3 — Passive Voice

Ketika tindakan dilakukan terhadap subject.

Future Passive

will be + V3
  • The building will be completed next year.
  • The report will be submitted tomorrow.

Conditional Passive

would be + V3
  • The candidate would be hired if he passed the interview.

15 WILL HAVE BEEN + V3 — Future Perfect Passive

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan bahwa sesuatu akan sudah diselesaikan pada waktu tertentu di masa depan.

will have been + V3
  • By next year, the building will have been completed.
Struktur: will + have + been + V3

16 Will Have Been + V-ing vs V3 — Jangan Tertukar

Future Perfect ContinuousFuture Perfect Passive
will have been + V-ing
Menekankan durasi aktivitas.
I will have been working for ten years.		 will have been + V3
Menekankan tindakan yang diterima subject.
The building will have been completed.

18 PAST CONTINUOUS — WAS/WERE + V-ING

Digunakan untuk menyatakan kegiatan yang sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa lalu.

Rumus

Subject + was/were + V-ing

Contoh

  • The manager was preparing the report when the client called.
  • Manajer sedang mempersiapkan laporan ketika klien menelepon.

Pola penting TOEFL

Past Continuous + when + Simple Past
  • She was studying when her friend called.
Cara mengenali pola was studying menunjukkan kegiatan yang sedang berlangsung, sedangkan called menunjukkan kejadian yang terjadi atau menyela kegiatan tersebut.

🎯 Cara Cepat Mengerjakan Soal

Saat menemukan soal will/would, lakukan 3 langkah:

1

Lihat waktunya

tomorrow / next week → kemungkinan will  |  this time tomorrow → will be + V-ing  |  by next year → will have + V3

2

Lihat IF

If + V1 → will + V1  |  If + V2 → would + V1  |  If + had + V3 → would have + V3

3

Lihat konteks masa lalu

Jika ada said, thought, believed, knew, expected → perhatikan kemungkinan perubahan will → would. Contoh: She said that she would come.
