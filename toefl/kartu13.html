01 Present Perfect

Menunjukkan tindakan yang telah terjadi dan masih memiliki hubungan dengan keadaan sekarang.

Subject + have/has + V3

Scientists have developed several new methods.

The researcher has completed the experiment.

SubjectAuxiliary
I / You / We / They / Plural nounhave
He / She / It / Singular nounhas

02 Past Perfect

Menunjukkan tindakan yang terjadi lebih dahulu sebelum tindakan lain di masa lalu.

Subject + had + V3

The researchers had completed the experiment before the laboratory closed.

Urutan kejadianhad completed → laboratory closed
ArtinyaEksperimen selesai lebih dulu, sebelum lab tutup.

03 Future Perfect

Menunjukkan tindakan yang diperkirakan sudah selesai pada suatu waktu di masa depan.

Subject + will have + V3

Kata petunjuk yang sering muncul: by the time, by next year, by the end of...

By next June, the university will have completed the new building.

04 Subject–Verb Agreement

Bagian penting: tentukan subject sebenarnya sebelum memilih verb. Jangan memilih verb hanya berdasarkan kata yang paling dekat — cari subject utama terlebih dahulu.

The quality of the products has improved. (subject = quality, singular)

The products have been manufactured. (subject = products, plural)

05 Relative Clause dengan "That" / "Who"

Relative clause memberikan informasi tambahan tentang noun sebelumnya — verb di dalam klausa mengikuti noun yang diacunya.

The products that have been manufactured by the company... (products = plural)

products that has been manufactured

The product that has been manufactured... (product = singular)

06 Subject Utama dan Relative Clause Bisa Berbeda Verb

Dalam satu kalimat, subject utama dan noun di dalam relative clause bisa memiliki bentuk yang berbeda — sehingga have dan has bisa muncul sekaligus.

The quality of the products that have been manufactured by the company has improved.

Klausa dalamproducts → have been manufactured
Subject utamaquality → has improved

07 Conditional Sentence

Type 1 — kemungkinan realistis

If + V1, will + V1

If the technology improves, the company will expand its operations.

Type 2 — kondisi hipotetis sekarang/masa depan

If + V2, would + V1

If I had more time, I would study English.

Type 3 — kondisi yang tidak terjadi di masa lalu

If + had + V3, would have + V3

If the researchers had identified the problem, they would have prevented the accident.

08 Inversion — Second Conditional

Dalam TOEFL, if kadang dihilangkan dan struktur dibalik.

Normal: If the company were to increase production, it would be successful.

Inversion: Were the company to increase production, it would be successful.

Were + subject + to + V1 → would + V1

Were the university to receive more funding, it would be able to expand.

09 Inversion — Third Conditional

Normal: If the information had been available earlier, the committee would have made a different decision.

Inversion: Had the information been available earlier, the committee would have made a different decision.

Had + subject + V3 → would have + V3

Had the researchers recognized the warning signs, they would have prevented the accident.

Penting: setelah had dalam pola ini digunakan V3, bukan V1.

Had ... been

Had ... identified

Had ... be

Had ... identify

10 Inversion dengan "Not Until"

Ketika Not until diletakkan di awal kalimat, terjadi inversion pada klausa utama.

Not until the results were available did the scientists understand the significance of the discovery.

Not until the results were available the scientists understood...

Not until + clause + did + subject + V1

Perhatikan bahwa setelah did, verb kembali ke V1: did + understand ✓, bukan did + understood ✗.

11 Neither...Nor

Dalam struktur Neither A nor B + verb, verb mengikuti subject yang paling dekat dengan verb.

Neither the professor nor the students have arrived. (students = terdekat, plural)

Neither the students nor the professor has arrived. (professor = terdekat, singular)

Strategi Mengerjakan TOEFL Structure

Saat menemukan soal grammar, lakukan tiga langkah:

  1. Cari subject utama.
  2. Tentukan apakah kalimat berbicara tentang sekarang, masa lalu, atau masa depan.
  3. Periksa apakah ada kata atau struktur khusus (lihat tabel di bawah).
