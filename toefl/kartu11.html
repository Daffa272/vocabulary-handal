RANGKUMAN MATERI

Menguasai pola kalimat yang diuji di TOEFL Structure.

Soal Structure tidak menguji makna kalimat, melainkan pola. Begitu sebuah pola dikenali — modal, passive, clause, atau inversion — jawaban yang benar biasanya sudah bisa ditebak dalam beberapa detik.

5Pola paling penting
1Ringkasan cepat
01

Modal + Verb

Modal yang sering muncul: can, could, may, might, should, must, will, would. Setelah modal, kata kerja selalu dalam bentuk dasar (V1).

POLA
Subject + modal + V1
Students can improve their English.
People could travel more easily in the past.
❌ can improved · can improving · can to improve ✅ can improve
02

Modal + Passive Voice

Passive dipakai ketika subjek menerima tindakan, bukan melakukannya.

POLA
Subject + modal + be + V3
The problem can be solved easily.
The documents could be accessed online.
ActivePassive
Technology can improve education.Education can be improved by technology.
can + V1 → can + be + V3 could + V1 → could + be + V3 should + V1 → should + be + V3 must + V1 → must + be + V3
03

Modal Perfect

Dipakai untuk membicarakan kemungkinan atau kemampuan yang berkaitan dengan masa lalu.

AKTIF
S + could + have + V3
PASIF
S + could + have + been + V3
She could have finished the work.
Dia mungkin/dapat saja telah menyelesaikan pekerjaan tersebut.
The accident could have been prevented.
Kecelakaan tersebut sebenarnya dapat dicegah.
Urutan wajib: could → have → been → V3. Jangan sampai terbalik atau ada kata yang hilang.
❌ could been prevented · could have prevent · could have been prevent ✅ could have been prevented
04

Subject + Modal + Adjective

Modal tidak selalu diikuti verb — jika kata berikutnya adalah adjective, gunakan "be" sebelum kata sifat itu.

POLA
Subject + modal + be + adjective
The city can be beautiful.
The information could be useful.
The problem may be difficult.
VerbAdjective
Technology can improve education.Technology can be useful.
05

Subject–Verb Agreement

Subject dan verb harus sesuai jumlahnya — jangan tertipu kata yang berada di antara keduanya.

SINGULAR
He / She / It + V-s/es
PLURAL
They / We / People + V1
She works every day. · The machine produces electricity.
They work every day. · People use technology.
The quality of the products is excellent — subjek sebenarnya adalah "the quality", bukan "products".
06

Gerund vs Infinitive

Gerund (V-ing) berfungsi seperti noun; infinitive (to + V1) mengikuti verb tertentu.

GERUND
Verb + ing
INFINITIVE
to + V1
Reading is important. · I enjoy reading books.
I want to study English. · They decided to leave early.
enjoyavoidfinishconsidersuggestpractice
wantneeddecidehopeplanpromiseexpect
07

Parallel Structure

Kata atau frasa yang sejajar dalam satu kalimat harus memakai bentuk grammar yang sama.

She likes reading, writing, and speaking English.
The job requires patience, skill, and dedication.
❌ reading, writing, and to speak ✅ reading, writing, and speaking
andorbutboth...andeither...orneither...nornot only...but also
08

Reduced Adjective Clause

Adjective clause bisa direduksi agar kalimat lebih ringkas, tanpa mengubah makna.

Bentuk lengkapDireduksi
The man who works here...The man working here...
The books which are written in English...The books written in English...
AKTIF
who/which + V → V-ing
PASIF
who/which + be + V3 → V3
09

Noun Clause

Clause yang berfungsi sebagai noun. Susunan kata di dalamnya bukan bentuk pertanyaan.

I know what he wants.
She explained why the meeting was canceled.
We don't know where they live.
Direct questionNoun clause
Where does he live?I know where he lives.
whatwherewhenwhyhowwhetherifthat
10

Inversion

Perubahan posisi auxiliary/modal dan subject, dipicu oleh kata negatif tertentu di awal kalimat.

POLA
Negative expression + auxiliary + subject + verb
NormalInversion
The students rarely do this.Rarely do the students do this.
Never have I seen such a beautiful place.
neverrarelyseldomhardlyscarcelybarelynot onlyunder no circumstances
11

Participial Phrase

Memberikan informasi tambahan tentang subject kalimat utama.

PRESENT PARTICIPLE
V-ing
PAST PARTICIPLE
V3
Walking along the street, she saw an old friend.
Saat berjalan di sepanjang jalan, dia melihat seorang teman lama.
Located near the airport, the hotel is convenient for travelers.
Terletak dekat bandara, hotel tersebut nyaman bagi wisatawan.
Subjek dari participial phrase harus sesuai dengan subjek kalimat utama.
12

Modal Perfect Passive

Kombinasi yang sangat sering muncul di TOEFL Structure.

UTAMA
Modal + be + V3
MASA LALU
Modal + have + been + V3
The problem must be solved immediately.
The information should be provided clearly.
The problem should have been solved earlier.
The accident could have been prevented.
13

Perfect Tense

Present perfect menghubungkan kejadian lampau dengan sekarang; past perfect menandai kejadian yang lebih dulu terjadi.

PRESENT PERFECT
have/has + V3
PAST PERFECT
had + V3
She has finished her work.
They have lived here for ten years.
The train had left before we arrived.
Urutan kejadian: kejadian pertama pakai had + V3, kejadian berikutnya pakai V2 biasa.
14

So...that

Menunjukkan hasil atau akibat dari suatu sifat atau keadaan.

ADJECTIVE
so + adjective + that
ADVERB
so + adverb + that
The book was so interesting that I read it twice.
He ran so quickly that nobody could catch him.
15

Such...that

Fungsinya sama seperti so...that, tapi diikuti noun phrase, bukan adjective/adverb langsung.

POLA
such + noun phrase + that
It was such an interesting book that I read it twice.
SOSUCH
so + adjective/adverb + thatsuch + noun + that
