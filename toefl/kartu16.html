Rangkuman Materi TOEFL — Structure

Conjunctions & Connectors

Panduan lengkap tentang kata penghubung dalam bahasa Inggris — coordinating, subordinating, dan paired conjunctions — lengkap dengan pola kalimat dan trik cepat untuk soal TOEFL Structure.

01Apa itu Conjunction?

Conjunction adalah kata yang menghubungkan kata, frasa, atau klausa. Dalam soal TOEFL Structure, conjunction biasanya digunakan untuk menghubungkan dua klausa dan menunjukkan hubungan makna di antara keduanya.

I work and study.
She stayed home because she was sick.

02Coordinating Conjunctions

Pola utama: Independent Clause + conjunction + Independent Clause

AND — dan (penambahan)

The manager explained the procedure and the employees followed it.
Manajer menjelaskan prosedurnya dan para karyawan mengikutinya.

BUT — tetapi (pertentangan)

The task was difficult, but the employees completed it.
The company is small, but it has many customers.

OR — atau (pilihan / konsekuensi)

You can call me or send me an email.
Submit the report today, or you may miss the deadline.
Ingat
A, or B → lakukan A, atau B akan terjadi.

SO — sehingga / jadi (hasil / akibat)

The road was crowded, so we arrived late.
Sebab → SO → akibat
The system was slow → so → the process took longer.

03Subordinating Conjunctions

Conjunction ini menghubungkan dependent clause dengan independent clause.

BECAUSE — karena (sebab / alasan)

The researcher conducted another experiment because the first results were inconclusive.
Effect + because + Cause

atau

Because + Cause, + Effect
Because the results were unclear, the researcher conducted another experiment.
Kata kunci
because → WHY?

04ALTHOUGH — meskipun

Menunjukkan pertentangan.

Although the project was difficult, the team completed it.
Meskipun proyeknya sulit, tim menyelesaikannya.
Although + contrasting idea, + main clause
Although the company is small, it has many customers.

05IF — jika

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan kondisi.

If you follow the instructions, you can complete the task easily.
If + condition, + result
If the data are accurate, the results will be reliable.
Ingat
IF = jika kondisi tersebut terjadi

06UNLESS — kecuali jika / jika tidak

Unless memiliki makna yang mirip dengan if ... not.

You will fail unless you study.

≈ You will fail if you do not study.

Unless + condition
Unless you submit the form, your application cannot be processed.

07BEFORE — sebelum

Menunjukkan urutan waktu.

Check the information before you submit the report.
Periksa informasinya sebelum mengirim laporan.
Before + event 1, event 2

08AFTER — setelah

Menunjukkan sesuatu yang terjadi setelah kejadian lain.

After the employees received training, their productivity increased.
Training → Productivity increased

09WHILE — ketika / sementara

Dapat menunjukkan dua hal — perhatikan konteks kalimat.

A. Dua aktivitas berlangsung bersamaan

She studied while her brother watched television.

B. Pertentangan

Some people prefer working independently, while others prefer teamwork.

10SO THAT — sehingga / agar

Menunjukkan tujuan atau hasil yang diharapkan.

The manager explained the procedure clearly so that everyone could understand it.
Manajer menjelaskan prosedurnya dengan jelas agar semua orang dapat memahaminya.
Bedakan
SO → hasil/akibat: It was raining, so we stayed home.
SO THAT → tujuan: He spoke loudly so that everyone could hear him.

11THAT sebagai Noun Clause

That dapat memperkenalkan klausa yang berfungsi sebagai objek atau pelengkap.

I believe that practical experience is important.
Subject + Verb + that + Subject + Verb
I know that the company needs new employees.
She explained that the results were accurate.
Perhatikan
I believe that practical experience is essential. — bukan I believe although practical experience...
Setelah believe / know / think / consider, sering diperlukan noun clause.

12Paired Conjunctions

Conjunction yang digunakan berpasangan.

EITHER ... OR — salah satu ... atau ...

You can either work from home or work at the office.
Either + A + or + B

NEITHER ... NOR — tidak ... dan tidak juga ...

Neither the manager nor the employees were prepared.
Baik manajer maupun karyawan tidak siap.
Ingat
Neither A nor B = A dan B sama-sama tidak

BOTH ... AND — keduanya ... dan ...

Both the manager and the employees attended the meeting.

NOT ONLY ... BUT ALSO — tidak hanya ... tetapi juga ...

The program provides not only theoretical knowledge but also practical experience.
Not only + A + but also + B

WHETHER ... OR — apakah ... atau ...

Digunakan ketika terdapat dua kemungkinan.

I am not sure whether the company will accept my application or ask for additional documents.
Whether + A + or + B
Bedakan Either...Or dengan Whether...Or
Either...or → pilihan: You can either stay here or leave.
Whether...or → ketidakpastian dua kemungkinan: I don't know whether he will stay or leave.

13Cara Menentukan Conjunction dengan Cepat

Saat mengerjakan TOEFL, jangan langsung menerjemahkan semua kalimat. Cari hubungan logis terlebih dahulu, lalu tanyakan pada diri sendiri:

  1. Apakah menunjukkan sebab?
    because — "Why did it happen? → Because..."
  2. Apakah menunjukkan pertentangan?
    although / but / while
  3. Apakah menunjukkan pilihan?
    or / either...or
  4. Apakah menunjukkan dua kemungkinan yang belum pasti?
    whether...or
  5. Apakah keduanya sama-sama tidak?
    neither...nor
  6. Apakah menunjukkan kondisi?
    if
  7. Apakah menunjukkan pengecualian kondisi?
    unless
  8. Apakah menunjukkan urutan waktu?
    before / after / while
  9. Apakah menunjukkan tujuan?
    so that
  10. Apakah setelah kata kerja seperti believe, know, think, consider?
    that + clause
