01Subject–Verb Agreement

Verb harus sesuai dengan jumlah subject.

Singular → verb singular

She is a good manager.
The company has a new policy.

Plural → verb plural

They are employees.
The students have assignments.

Jangan tertipu oleh kata di tengah

The quality of the products is excellent.
Subject sebenarnya adalah quality (singular), bukan products.
SubjectBeHave
Iamhave
He / She / Itishas
You / We / Theyarehave

02Simple Present

Digunakan untuk fakta, kebiasaan, kondisi umum, dan jadwal.

S + V1  |  He/She/It + V-s/es
I work every day.
She works every day.

Setelah when / before / after / until, jika kalimat utama membicarakan masa depan, klausa waktu tetap memakai Simple Present.

I will call you when I arrive.
when I will arrive

03Simple Past

Untuk kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu.

S + V2
I visited Japan last year.
She was in Jakarta yesterday.

Kata petunjuk: yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, when I was young.

04Present Continuous

Untuk sesuatu yang sedang berlangsung sekarang.

S + am/is/are + V-ing
I am reading a book.
She is working now.
They are studying.

05Past Continuous sudah kuat

Untuk aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa lalu — salah satu pola yang muncul di soalmu.

S + was/were + V-ing
I was reading when you called.
They were working when the manager arrived.

Pola TOEFL yang sering muncul:

While + S + was/were + V-ing
While I was studying, my friend called me.

06Present Perfect sudah kuat

S + have/has + V3

Digunakan untuk kejadian yang dimulai di masa lalu dan masih berkaitan dengan sekarang, pengalaman, atau kejadian yang sudah terjadi.

They have lived in Jakarta since 2020.
She has finished the assignment.

since + titik waktu (since 2020, since Monday) — for + durasi (for three years, for two months).

07Past Perfect prioritas utama

Salah satu materi yang perlu kamu perkuat. Digunakan ketika ada dua kejadian di masa lalu, untuk menunjukkan kejadian mana yang terjadi lebih dahulu.

S + had + V3
When I arrived, she had left.
Urutan: she left → I arrived. Karena "left" terjadi lebih dahulu, dipakai "had left".

Pola TOEFL:

By the time + S + V2, S + had + V3
By the time we arrived, the train had left.

Cara berpikir: kalau ada "By the time...", cek apakah ada dua kejadian masa lalu. Kejadian pertama → had + V3, kejadian kedua → V2.

08Future dengan Will sudah kuat

S + will + V1
The company will open a new branch.
I will help you.

Setelah will, selalu gunakan V1.
will opened, will opening — will open

09Be Going To

Untuk rencana atau sesuatu yang diperkirakan akan terjadi.

am/is/are + going to + V1
I am going to study tonight.
She is going to leave tomorrow.
They are going to build a new office.

10Future in the Past prioritas utama

Salah satu materi yang paling perlu kamu kuasai. Artinya "akan", tetapi dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu.

FutureFuture in the Past

Direct: He said, "I will come."
Reported: He said that he would come.
I will receive the scholarship if I pass.
Dilihat dari masa lalu: I was going to receive the scholarship if I passed.

11Sequence of Tenses perlu latihan

Ketika kata kerja utama berada di past tense, kata kerja di klausa berikutnya sering ikut mundur waktunya.

Bentuk asliSetelah dilaporkan

willwould
cancould
I am tired. → He said that he was tired.
He said, "I will help." → He said that he would help.
He said, "I can do it." → He said that he could do it.

12Passive Voice perlu latihan

Sangat sering muncul di soal TOEFL. Pada active voice, subject melakukan tindakan; pada passive voice, subject menerima tindakan.

Active: The security closed the doors.
Passive: The doors were closed by the security.

13Infinitive sudah kuat

to + V1

Contoh: to study, to work, to be, to help.

We wanted to be more independent.
She decided to leave early.

Pola penting: want / need / decide / plan / hope + to V1.
wanted being, wanted been — wanted to be

14Gerund

Gerund adalah V-ing yang berfungsi seperti noun.

Reading is important.
Working hard is necessary.

Setelah preposition, biasanya gunakan V-ing.

She is interested in learning English.
He is good at speaking English.

15There + Be

Jangan melihat kata there sebagai subject — perhatikan noun setelah be.

Singular: There is a problem.
Plural: There are several problems.

Contoh soal: There _____ several reasons for the increase. Jawaban: are, karena "reasons" plural.

16No Sooner...Than prioritas utama

No sooner had + S + V3 + than + S + V2
No sooner had he arrived than the meeting started.
Artinya: baru saja dia tiba, rapat langsung dimulai.

Perhatikan urutan: had arrived → started, bukan had arrived → starts.

17By the Time prioritas utama

By the time + S + V2, S + had + V3
By the time I arrived, she had left.
Maknanya: (1) she left, (2) I arrived.

Shortcut: kalau melihat "By the time...", cari dua kejadian masa lalu. Yang terjadi lebih dahulu biasanya had + V3.

Lima Rumus Terpenting

Past — had + V3 → V2
The train had left before we arrived.
Future — will + V1
I will study tomorrow.
Future in the Past — would + V1
He said he would study.
Future in the Past (rencana) — was/were going to + V1
She was going to study.
Passive — be + V3
The report was completed.
Infinitive — to + V1
They wanted to leave.
