PRONOUNS, NOUNS & SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT

Materi Grammar TOEFL — 25 Konsep Penting

🎯 Cara Dasar Mengerjakan TOEFL Structure

Sebelum memilih jawaban, tentukan terlebih dahulu Subject → Singular/Plural → Verb → Kecocokan.

1️⃣ Cari Subject
2️⃣ Tentukan Jumlah
3️⃣ Cari Verb
4️⃣ Cocokkan

1. Subject Pronoun

Subject pronoun digunakan sebagai subjek atau pelaku tindakan.

Person Singular Plural
1st I we
2nd you you
3rd he, she, it they
Contoh:
She is a professor.
They are students.
❌ Her is a professor.
✅ She is a professor.

2. Object Pronoun

Digunakan ketika pronoun menjadi objek verb atau preposition.

Subject Object
Ime
youyou
hehim
sheher
itit
weus
theythem
Contoh:
The teacher called me.
She invited us.

3. Subject vs Object

Tentukan apakah pronoun berfungsi sebagai subjek atau objek.

Pelaku tindakan → SUBJECT
Penerima tindakan → OBJECT
My sister and I went to school.

The teacher spoke to my sister and me.

4. Possessive Adjective

Menunjukkan kepemilikan dan biasanya diikuti noun.

Subject Possessive
Imy
youyour
hehis
sheher
itits
weour
theytheir
Possessive Adjective + Noun
my book
her car
their project

5. Possessive Pronoun

Menunjukkan kepemilikan tetapi tidak diikuti noun.

Adjective Pronoun
mymine
youryours
hishis
herhers
ourours
theirtheirs
This is my book.
This book is mine.

6. Reflexive Pronoun

Digunakan ketika subject dan object merujuk pada orang yang sama.

Subject Reflexive
Imyself
youyourself
hehimself
sheherself
ititself
weourselves
theythemselves
He blamed himself.
She introduced herself.
They hurt themselves.

7. Dummy Subject "It"

"It" dapat digunakan sebagai subjek formal untuk keadaan, waktu, atau situasi.

It + be + adjective + to + verb
It is important to study.
It is difficult to understand.
It is necessary to exercise.

8. There + Be

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan keberadaan sesuatu.

There is + singular noun
There are + plural noun
There is a problem.
There are several problems.

9. Subject–Verb Agreement

Singular Subject → Singular Verb
Plural Subject → Plural Verb
The student works hard.
The students work hard.

10. He / She / It + Verb

Dalam simple present, he/she/it biasanya membutuhkan verb + s/es.

She works every day.
He studies English.
It looks good.
❌ She work every day.
✅ She works every day.

11. HAS vs HAVE

He / She / It → HAS
I / You / We / They → HAVE
She has a car.
They have a car.

12. Collective Noun

Noun yang menunjukkan kelompok dan biasanya dianggap singular dalam pola TOEFL American English.

The team is ready.
The committee has made a decision.
The company is successful.

13. Compound Subject

Dua subject yang dihubungkan dengan and biasanya menjadi plural.

A + AND + B → PLURAL
My father and my brother work together.
Tom and Jerry are friends.

14. EACH + Singular

Each + singular noun + singular verb
Each student is responsible.
Each manager has a phone.

15. NEITHER + Singular

Neither + singular noun + singular verb
Neither answer is correct.
Neither student was present.

16. EVERY + Singular

Every + singular noun + singular verb
Every student has a book.
Every employee needs training.

17. SOME Bisa Singular / Plural

Perhatikan noun setelah "some".

Some students are absent.

Some information is missing.

18. Countable vs Uncountable

Countable

student, book, car, problem

two students
several problems
many books

Uncountable

information, advice, news, equipment, research

some information
some advice
some equipment

19. A vs ANY

A + singular countable noun
ANY + plural / uncountable noun
I have a problem.
Do you have any questions?
Do you have any information?

20. Noun Phrase

Noun phrase adalah kelompok kata yang berfungsi sebagai noun.

The research team completed the project.

The paintings by Pedro Reiss are beautiful.

21. Head Noun

Head noun adalah noun utama yang menentukan singular/plural dan verb.

The paintings by Pedro Reiss are beautiful.

The quality of the products is excellent.

22. "OF + NOUN" sebagai Distractor

Jangan tertipu oleh noun yang muncul setelah "of".

Each of the students is ready.

The quality of the products is excellent.
Verb mengikuti SUBJECT / HEAD NOUN, bukan noun setelah "of".

23. Singular Noun Berakhiran -S

Beberapa noun terlihat plural tetapi sebenarnya singular.

Physics is difficult.
Economics is interesting.
The news is surprising.

24. Noun Bergantung Konteks

Beberapa kata seperti politics dan statistics dapat berubah berdasarkan makna.

Politics is complicated.

Statistics is an important subject.

The statistics are surprising.

25. Plural Nouns

Noun plural membutuhkan plural verb.

The students are studying.
The employees have a meeting.
The managers are responsible.
The paintings were expensive.

⭐ 10 Rumus TOEFL yang Wajib Dikuasai

1. He / She / It + V-s
→ She works.
2. I / You / We / They + V1
→ They work.
3. He / She / It + HAS
→ She has a car.
4. I / You / We / They + HAVE
→ They have cars.
5. Each + singular noun + singular verb
→ Each student is ready.
6. Every + singular noun + singular verb
→ Every student has a book.
7. Neither + singular noun + singular verb
→ Neither answer is correct.
8. There is + singular / There are + plural
→ There is a problem.
→ There are many problems.
9. A + B → plural
→ Tom and Jerry are friends.
10. Subject + of + noun → Verb mengikuti subject
→ The quality of the products is excellent.
