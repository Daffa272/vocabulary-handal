TOEFL STRUCTURE: LATIHAN FINAL

25 Materi Penting + 25 Latihan Soal & Pembahasan Lengkap

📚 Bagian A: Materi TOEFL Structure

1. Comparative Adjective

Digunakan untuk membandingkan dua hal atau dua orang.

Short adjective + -er + than
Long adjective → more + adjective + than
✅ This method is more efficient than the traditional method.
❌ This method is more efficient that the traditional method.
💡 Kata than digunakan setelah comparative.

2. Subject–Verb Agreement

Verb harus sesuai dengan jumlah subject.

Singular → V-s / is / has
Plural → V1 / are / have
✅ The number of students is increasing.
❌ The number of students are increasing.
💡 The number of + plural noun dianggap singular.

3. Relative Clause

Digunakan untuk memberikan informasi tambahan tentang noun.

who → orang
whom → orang sebagai object
which → benda/hewan
whose → kepemilikan
where → tempat
✅ The scientist who discovered the medicine won an award.
❌ The scientist which discovered the medicine...

4. Many of Whom

Digunakan untuk merujuk kepada orang sebagai object setelah "many of".

many of whom + clause
✅ Many of whom came from overseas.
💡 Untuk benda biasanya digunakan many of which.

5. Reduced Adjective Clause

Relative clause dapat dipersingkat.

Active → noun + V-ing
Passive → noun + V3
✅ The aircraft tested last month is efficient.
❌ The aircraft testing last month is efficient.

6. Gerund (V-ing)

Gerund adalah verb-ing yang berfungsi sebagai noun.

Subject + V-ing | Verb + V-ing
✅ I recommend analyzing the data.
❌ I recommend to analyze the data.
💡 recommend, suggest, avoid, enjoy → biasanya diikuti V-ing.

7. Gerund setelah Preposition

Setelah preposition, gunakan V-ing.

Preposition + V-ing
✅ She succeeded in solving the problem.
❌ She succeeded in solve the problem.
💡 in, on, at, by, without, after, before + V-ing.

8. Infinitive (to + V1)

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan tujuan atau mengikuti kata tertentu.

to + V1
✅ The software allows users to process data.
❌ The software allows users to processing data.

9. In Order To

Menunjukkan tujuan.

in order to + V1
✅ They built the road in order to improve transportation.
❌ They built the road in order improving transportation.

10. Noun Clause

Noun clause berfungsi sebagai noun dalam kalimat.

how / what / when / where / whether / if + clause
✅ I know what he wants.
❌ I know what does he want.
💡 Noun clause menggunakan susunan statement, bukan question.

11. Whether

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan pilihan atau ketidakpastian.

whether + subject + verb
✅ Scientists are studying whether the treatment is effective.

12. It Was Not Until ... That

Struktur khusus untuk menekankan waktu.

It was not until + time + that + clause
✅ It was not until the twentieth century that scientists understood DNA.
❌ It was not until the twentieth century scientists understood DNA.

13. Despite vs Although

despite + noun / V-ing
although + subject + verb
✅ They succeeded despite several problems.
✅ They succeeded although the system was difficult.
❌ Despite the system was difficult...

14. Passive Voice

Passive menekankan tindakan atau objek yang dikenai tindakan.

be + V3
✅ The data was collected yesterday.
❌ The data was collect yesterday.
💡 Past participle/V3 adalah ciri utama passive voice.

15. Past Perfect

had + V3
✅ The storm had ended before they arrived.
💡 Digunakan untuk kejadian yang terjadi lebih dahulu di masa lalu.

16. Present Perfect

has/have + V3
✅ The number of users has increased.
❌ The number of users have increased.

17. The More ..., The More ...

Menunjukkan hubungan perbandingan sebab-akibat.

The + comparative, the + comparative
✅ The more you practice, the better you become.

18. Correlative Conjunctions

Menghubungkan dua elemen yang sejajar.

both ... and
either ... or
neither ... nor
not only ... but also
✅ She is both intelligent and hardworking.
❌ She is both intelligent and works hard.

19. Adjective vs Adverb

Adjective → menjelaskan noun
Adverb → menjelaskan verb/adjective
✅ She is a careful driver.
✅ She drives carefully.
❌ She drives careful.

20. Where Relative Adverb

Where digunakan untuk menunjukkan tempat.

place + where + subject + verb
✅ This is the city where she was born.
❌ This is the city where she was born in.

21. Neither ... Nor

Verb mengikuti subject yang paling dekat.

Neither A nor B + verb
✅ Neither the manager nor the employees are available.
❌ Neither the manager nor the employees is available.

22. Future Tense

will + V1
✅ The amount of waste will increase.
❌ The amount of waste will increases.

23. Parallel Structure

Elemen yang sejajar harus mempunyai bentuk grammar yang sama.

V-ing + V-ing + V-ing (contoh)
✅ Students enjoy reading, writing, and communicating.
❌ Students enjoy reading, writing, and communicate.

24. Adjective / Adverb Comparison

Adjective → -er / more
Adverb → -er / more
✅ She is more careful than before.
✅ She works more carefully now.

25. Preposition + Noun / V-ing

Setelah preposition dapat digunakan noun atau gerund.

preposition + noun / V-ing
✅ He left without saying goodbye.
❌ He left without say goodbye.
