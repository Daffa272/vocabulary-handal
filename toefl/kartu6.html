MATERI TOEFL

Adverbs & Related Grammar

Kuasai penggunaan kata keterangan (Adverbs), posisi dalam kalimat, hingga perbedaan dengan Adjective untuk menaklukkan soal struktur TOEFL.

1

Apa itu Adverb?

Adverb adalah kata keterangan yang memberikan informasi tambahan tentang suatu kejadian, tindakan, atau sifat.

Adverb menjelaskan:

  • Bagaimana tindakan dilakukan
  • Kapan terjadi
  • Di mana terjadi
  • Seberapa sering
  • Seberapa besar/tingkatnya
  • Kemungkinan terjadinya sesuatu

Contoh:

She speaks slowly.

Dia berbicara dengan lambat.

slowly menjelaskan bagaimana speaks dilakukan → Adverb of Manner.

2

Jenis-Jenis Adverb (Wajib Dikuasai)

Jenis Fungsi / Menjawab Contoh
Manner Bagaimana? (How?) slowly, carefully, quickly, safely
Time Kapan? (When?) today, yesterday, soon, recently
Frequency Seberapa sering? always, usually, often, never
Degree Seberapa besar? (How much?) very, really, greatly, extremely
Probability Kemungkinan (How likely?) probably, possibly, certainly
Place Di mana? (Where?) here, there, everywhere, outside
Manner

Bagaimana?

Pola: Subject + Verb + Adverb

  • The alarm caused the group to move rashly.
  • They worked carefully.
Time

Kapan?

Tips: Jika soal menunjukkan keterangan waktu, kemungkinan besar jawabannya Adverb of Time.
  • Today, everything seems to change in split seconds.
Frequency

Seberapa sering?

Urutan: always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never

  • Financial advisers generally recommend saving money.
Degree & Prob.

Tingkat & Kemungkinan

  • Their lives greatly improved. (Degree)
  • He will probably come. (Probability)
⚠️ Salah: He will probable come. (Probable = Adjective)
3

Adjective vs Adverb ⭐⭐⭐

Ini adalah konsep yang SANGAT PENTING untuk soal Structure TOEFL.

ADJECTIVE (Kata Sifat)

Menjelaskan Noun (Kata Benda) atau Pronoun.

She is a careful driver.

"careful" menjelaskan noun "driver".

ADVERB (Kata Keterangan)

Menjelaskan Verb, Adjective, atau Adverb lain.

She drives carefully.

"carefully" menjelaskan verb "drives".

Cara Membentuk Adverb

Umumnya: Adjective + -ly = Adverb

safe → safely
general → generally
direct → directly
complete → completely

⚠️ HATI-HATI Pengecualian!

Tidak semua adverb berakhiran -ly. Beberapa bentuknya sama persis dengan Adjective.

fast, hard, late, early, well

Contoh benar: He runs fast. (Bukan fastly)

4

Posisi & Pola Adverb ⭐⭐⭐

Posisi penempatan Adverb sangat sering diuji dalam soal susunan (Structure).

1. Adverb of Frequency (always, usually, dll)

  • Sebelum Main Verb:
    She always studies English.
  • Setelah To Be (is, am, are):
    She is always busy.
  • Setelah Modal (can, will, dll):
    He will probably come.
Contoh Soal: We cannot ____ stay in the old time.
✅ Benar: cannot always be (Modal + Adverb + Verb)

2. Pola kombinasi kata yang umum

Adverb + Verb

We usually study.

Adverb + Adjective

extremely effective

Adverb + Adverb

very quickly

QUICK TOEFL TRICK 🎯

Saat menemui soal rumpang (bagian kosong) terkait kelas kata, gunakan langkah ini:

STEP 1: Apa yang dijelaskan oleh kata kosong tersebut?

  • Jika menjelaskan NOUN → Butuh ADJECTIVE
  • Jika menjelaskan VERB → Butuh ADVERB
  • Jika menjelaskan ADJ/ADV lain → Butuh ADVERB

STEP 2: Pastikan maknanya dengan bertanya:

How? (Manner) • When? (Time) • Where? (Place) • How often? (Frequency) • How much? (Degree) • How likely? (Probability)

5

Grammar Terkait Lainnya

Parallel Structure ⭐⭐⭐

Jika ada deretan A, B, and C, bentuk kelas katanya harus setara (sejajar).

✅ effective, affordable, and reliable.
(Adj + Adj + Adj)

❌ effective, affordability, and reliable.
(Adj + Noun + Adj)

Preposition of Time

  • IN Tahun, Bulan, Abad (Waktu luas)
    in 1995, in March
  • ON Hari, Tanggal spesifik
    on Monday, on March 10
  • AT Jam, Waktu spesifik
    at 7 p.m., at noon
