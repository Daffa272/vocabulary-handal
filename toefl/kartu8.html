📚 TOEFL Structure: Verb & Tenses

Panduan Lengkap untuk Mengenali dan Menggunakan Tense dengan Tepat

🎯 Konsep Dasar: Cari "Verb Utama" Dulu

Dalam soal TOEFL Structure, langkah pertama adalah melihat bagian yang kosong:

The company _____ three new branches since it expanded.

Jangan langsung melihat semua kata. Tanyakan:

  • "Kata kerja apa yang dibutuhkan?"
  • Cek 3 hal:
    • Subject → siapa yang melakukan?
    • Time signal → kapan terjadinya?
    • Konteks → kejadian berlangsung, sudah selesai, atau masih berlangsung?

Contoh:

The company _____ three new branches since 2022.

Subject = The company

Time signal = since 2022

Maka: The company has opened three new branches since 2022. ✅

2️⃣ Simple Present

Fungsi

Digunakan untuk:

  • kebiasaan
  • fakta
  • kondisi umum
  • aktivitas yang berulang
  • sesuatu yang biasanya terjadi

Rumus

SubjectVerb
I/You/We/TheyV1
He/She/ItV1 + s/es

They work.

She works.

Time signal

  • every day
  • every morning
  • usually
  • generally
  • often
  • always
  • normally
  • regularly

Contoh dari latihan:

The professor usually _____ students to use reliable sources.

(A) encourage    (B) encouraged    (C) has encouraged    (D) encourages ✅

Ada usually → Simple Present.

Subject = The professor → singular.

Maka verb harus: encourages

⚠️ Jebakan TOEFL:

Jangan lupa -s/-es.

The company develops software. ✅

The companies develop software. ✅

3️⃣ Simple Past

Fungsi

Digunakan untuk kejadian yang:

  • terjadi di masa lalu
  • sudah selesai

Rumus

Subject + V2

The company launched a new product.

Time signal

  • yesterday
  • last week / last year / last month
  • in 2020 / in 2019
  • two years ago
  • earlier this year (jika periode yang dimaksud sudah selesai)

Contoh:

The company _____ its first sustainability report in 2021.

(A) publishes    (B) has published    (C) published ✅    (D) is publishing

in 2021 → waktu spesifik yang sudah lewat.

Jadi: published

4️⃣ Present Perfect

Ini salah satu tense paling penting dalam soal TOEFL.

Rumus

SubjectAuxiliary
I/You/We/Theyhave + V3
He/She/Ithas + V3

They have developed a new system.

The company has developed a new system.

Kapan menggunakan Present Perfect?

A. Since

  • since 2020
  • since Monday
  • since January
  • since the company was founded

The company has operated since 2020.

Maknanya: mulai 2020 dan masih relevan/berlangsung sampai sekarang.

B. For

  • for five years
  • for several months
  • for decades
  • for a long time

Scientists have studied climate change for decades.

C. Recently

Researchers have discovered several new applications recently.

D. Over the past...

  • over the past decade
  • over the past few years

Digital payment usage has increased over the past decade.

Ini adalah jebakan nomor 15 yang tadi kamu salah.

5️⃣ Bedakan Simple Past vs Present Perfect

Ini wajib banget dikuasai.

Simple Past

Waktu sudah selesai dan spesifik.

The company opened a branch last year.

Present Perfect

Periode masa lalu masih terhubung dengan sekarang.

The company has opened several branches over the past decade.

Perbandingan

Simple PastPresent Perfect
yesterdayrecently
last yearsince 2020
last monthfor five years
in 2020over the past decade
two years agoso far
in 2019until now
🚀 Shortcut TOEFL:
Kalau melihat: since / for / recently / over the past...
langsung curigai: HAVE/HAS + V3

6️⃣ Present Continuous

Rumus

Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

I am working.

She is working.

They are working.

Fungsi

Sesuatu yang sedang terjadi sekarang.

Time signal

  • now
  • currently
  • at the moment
  • right now
  • these days (tergantung konteks)

Contoh:

Currently, the government _____ several programs.

(A) develops    (B) developed    (C) has developed    (D) is developing ✅

Ada: Currently → sedang berlangsung.

7️⃣ Past Continuous

Rumus

was/were + V-ing

SubjectAuxiliary
I/He/She/Itwas
You/We/Theywere

She was working.

They were working.

Pola TOEFL yang sangat sering

  • While + Past Continuous
  • When + kejadian yang memotong

While the company was preparing the report, the CEO called.

When the manager arrived, the employees were preparing the report.

Artinya: aktivitas sedang berlangsung → kejadian lain terjadi.

Contoh:

When the electricity went out, the engineers _____ the system.

Jawaban: were testing ✅

Karena testing sedang berlangsung ketika listrik mati.

8️⃣ Past Perfect

Rumus

Subject + had + V3

Semua subject menggunakan had.

I had worked.

She had worked.

They had worked.

Fungsi

Ada dua kejadian di masa lalu, dan kita ingin menunjukkan mana yang terjadi lebih dulu.

Pola

Past Perfect → Simple Past

The auditors arrived.

The accounting department had prepared the documents.

Urutannya:

  1. Department prepared documents
  2. Auditors arrived

Maka:

By the time the auditors arrived, the accounting department had prepared all the documents. ✅

9️⃣ Before dan By the Time

Ini sering menjadi sinyal Past Perfect.

Before

Before the meeting started, the director had reviewed the report.

Urutan: had reviewed → meeting started

By the time

By the time the manager arrived, the employees had finished the work.

Artinya pekerjaan sudah selesai sebelum manager datang.

🚀 Shortcut:
Kalau menemukan: By the time + V2
periksa kemungkinan: had + V3

🔟 Future Simple

Rumus

will + V1

The company will expand next year.

Time signal

  • tomorrow
  • next week / next year
  • in the future
  • by 2030 (hati-hati, bisa Future Perfect)

Contoh:

The government _____ a new policy next year.

→ will introduce ✅

1️⃣1️⃣ Future Perfect

Ini level sedikit lebih tinggi.

Rumus

will have + V3

The company will have completed the project by 2030.

Artinya: pada 2030, pekerjaan tersebut sudah selesai.

Signal

  • by + future time

by 2030

by next year

by the end of this year

by next month

Contoh:

By the end of this year, the company _____ its carbon emissions by 30 percent.

Jawaban: will have reduced ✅

1️⃣2️⃣ Present Perfect Continuous

Rumus

have/has been + V-ing

Researchers have been developing new technology for many years.

Fungsi

Aktivitas:

  • dimulai di masa lalu
  • berlangsung selama periode tertentu
  • masih berlangsung atau baru saja berlangsung

Signal

Biasanya:

  • for + duration
  • since + starting point

Researchers have been developing new methods for many years.

🎯 Strategi Mengerjakan Soal TOEFL

Daripada membaca kalimat dari awal sampai akhir dan menerjemahkannya, gunakan 4 langkah.

STEP 1 — Cari subject

The number of electric vehicles _____ rapidly.

Subject sebenarnya: The number (Bukan: electric vehicles)

Karena the number = singular.

Maka: has increased ✅

STEP 2 — Cari time signal

Cari kata seperti:

since, for, recently, yesterday, last year, currently, usually, while, before, by

Ini sering langsung memberi tahu tense.

STEP 3 — Tentukan waktunya

Tanyakan: "Ini terjadi kapan?"

Since 2022 → mulai masa lalu → sekarang → Present Perfect

STEP 4 — Cocokkan bentuk verb

Contoh:

The company _____ software since 2020.

Pilihan: (A) develop   (B) develops   (C) has developed ✅   (D) is developing

since → have/has + V3

Jawaban: C

🔥 Cheat Sheet untuk Ujian

Kalau waktumu mepet, hafalkan bagian ini:

🟢 Simple Present
usually / generally / every / often → V1 / V1+s
The company operates every day.
🔵 Simple Past
yesterday / last / ago / in 2020 → V2
The company opened in 2020.
🟣 Present Perfect
since / for / recently / over the past → have/has + V3
The company has grown since 2020.
🟠 Present Continuous
now / currently / at the moment → am/is/are + V-ing
The company is growing rapidly.
🔴 Past Continuous
while / when + past event → was/were + V-ing
They were working when he arrived.
⚫ Past Perfect
before / by the time + past → had + V3
They had finished before he arrived.
🟡 Future Simple
tomorrow / next / future → will + V1
They will expand next year.
🟤 Future Perfect
by + future time → will have + V3
They will have expanded by 2030.

⚠️ 5 Jebakan yang Paling Perlu Kamu Waspadai

  1. Jangan tertipu noun yang dekat dengan verb

    The number of employees has increased. ✅

    Bukan: The number of employees have increased. ❌

  2. Since hampir selalu alarm Present Perfect

    Since 2020, the company has grown. ✅

    Bukan: Since 2020, the company grew. ❌ (untuk makna yang masih berlanjut)

  3. Last biasanya Simple Past

    Last year, researchers conducted a study. ✅

    Bukan: Last year, researchers have conducted a study. ❌

  4. Currently → lihat Continuous

    Currently, researchers are studying the issue. ✅

  5. By the time → cari urutan kejadian

    By the time he arrived, they had left.

    had left terjadi lebih dulu.
