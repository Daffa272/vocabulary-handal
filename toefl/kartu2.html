PRONOUNS, NOUNS & SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT

Materi Grammar TOEFL Lengkap

🎯 Fokus Utama TOEFL Structure

Dalam soal TOEFL Structure, jangan hanya mencari kata yang "terlihat benar". Tentukan terlebih dahulu fungsi kata, subject, jumlah noun, kemudian cocokkan dengan verb.

1. SUBJECT PRONOUN

Subject pronoun digunakan sebagai subjek atau orang/benda yang melakukan tindakan.

Person Singular Plural
1st I we
2nd you you
3rd he, she, it they
Contoh:

She has been proven right.
We were in a small rowing boat.
They are carrying on their traditions.
He made a speech.
❌ Her is a professor.
✅ She is a professor.

2. OBJECT PRONOUN

Object pronoun digunakan ketika pronoun menjadi objek dari verb atau preposition.

Subject Object
I me
you you
he him
she her
it it
we us
they them
The manager called me.
I saw him yesterday.
She invited us.
The teacher asked them to leave.

3. POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES

Menunjukkan kepemilikan dan biasanya harus diikuti noun.

Subject Possessive
I my
you your
he his
she her
it its
we our
they their
Possessive Adjective + Noun
my book
his car
her phone
our company
their project

The company completed its project.

4. POSSESSIVE PRONOUN

Possessive pronoun menunjukkan kepemilikan tetapi tidak diikuti noun.

Adjective Pronoun
my mine
your yours
his his
her hers
our ours
their theirs
This is my book.
This book is mine.
❌ This book is mine book.
❌ This book is my.
✅ This book is mine.

5. REFLEXIVE PRONOUN

Digunakan ketika subject dan object merujuk kepada orang yang sama.

Subject Reflexive
I myself
you yourself
he himself
she herself
it itself
we ourselves
they themselves
He blamed himself.
They blamed themselves.
She introduced herself.

6. DUMMY SUBJECT "IT"

"It" dapat menjadi subjek formal untuk menyatakan keadaan, fakta, waktu, atau situasi.

It + be + adjective + to + verb
It is important to study.
It is difficult to understand.
It is six hours from here.
It is a tragedy.
💡 Dalam pola ini, "it" bukan selalu berarti "itu". "It" dapat hanya berfungsi sebagai dummy subject.

7. THERE + BE

Digunakan untuk menunjukkan bahwa sesuatu atau seseorang ada/terdapat.

There is + singular noun
There are + plural noun
There is a problem.
There is a student in the room.
There are several problems.
There are twenty students in the room.
⚠️ Verb menyesuaikan noun setelah "there".

8. SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT

Subject dan verb harus sesuai dalam jumlah.

Singular Subject → Singular Verb
Plural Subject → Plural Verb
He works.
She has a car.
The company is successful.

They work.
The employees have meetings.
The companies are successful.

9. HE / SHE / IT + VERB

Dalam simple present, he/she/it biasanya membutuhkan tambahan -s / -es.

Dengan -s/-es Tanpa -s/-es
He works. I work.
She studies. We work.
It looks good. They work.
❌ She work every day.
✅ She works every day.

10. COLLECTIVE NOUN

Collective noun adalah noun yang menunjukkan kelompok, dan biasanya dianggap singular dalam pola TOEFL American English.

Contoh: team, company, committee, family, government, organization

The team is ready.
The company is successful.
The committee has made a decision.

11. EACH / EVERY / NEITHER

Each

Each + singular noun + singular verb
Each student is responsible.
Each manager has a phone.

Every

Every student has a book.
Every employee needs training.

Neither

Neither answer is correct.
Neither student was present.

12. SOME / MANY

Perhatikan noun yang mengikuti kata tersebut.

Some

Some students are absent.
Some information is missing.

Many

Many digunakan dengan plural countable noun.

Many students are absent.
Many problems remain unresolved.

13. INFINITIVE SEBAGAI SUBJECT

Infinitive adalah bentuk to + V1. Infinitive dapat berfungsi sebagai subject dalam kalimat.

To + V1 + ... + SINGULAR VERB
To study English is important.
To learn a language takes time.
To understand grammar requires practice.
To exercise every day is healthy.

Kenapa menggunakan singular verb?

Karena seluruh frasa infinitive dianggap sebagai satu aktivitas.

❌ To understand the problems require patience.
✅ To understand the problems requires patience.

14. INFINITIVE SEBAGAI OBJECT

Infinitive tidak selalu menjadi subject. Infinitive juga dapat menjadi object dari verb.

I want to study English.
She needs to improve her grammar.
They decided to leave early.
💡 Jika infinitive muncul setelah main verb seperti want, need, decide, hope, plan, dan expect, infinitive biasanya berfungsi sebagai object.

15. INFINITIVE vs GERUND

Bentuk Contoh Fungsi
Infinitive To study English is important. Subject
Gerund Studying English is important. Subject
Infinitive I want to study English. Object
⚠️ Yang harus diperhatikan dalam TOEFL bukan hanya bentuk katanya, tetapi fungsi kata dalam kalimat.

16. TRIK CEPAT MENGERJAKAN SOAL

Jika kalimat dimulai dengan "to + verb" → kemungkinan besar INFINITIVE sebagai SUBJECT.

Anggap seluruh infinitive phrase sebagai SATU aktivitas → gunakan SINGULAR VERB.
To study English is important.
To exercise every day is healthy.
To understand the problems requires patience.

17. IMPERATIVE / PERINTAH

Kalimat imperative biasanya menggunakan V1 / base form. Subject "you" biasanya tidak dituliskan.

V1 + ...
Use specific reasons and examples.
Explain your answer.
Read the passage carefully.
Identify the main idea.
❌ Explains your answer.
❌ Reads the passage carefully.
✅ Explain your answer.
✅ Read the passage carefully.

18. COMMON TOEFL TRAPS

Trap 1 — Noun setelah "of"

The quality of the products is excellent.

Subject = quality, bukan products.

Trap 2 — Noun phrase panjang

The paintings by Pedro Reiss are beautiful.

Head noun = paintings.

Trap 3 — Every / Each / Neither

Every student has a book.
Each applicant needs a reference.
Neither answer is correct.

Trap 4 — Infinitive subject

To learn English takes time.

Infinitive phrase dianggap singular.
