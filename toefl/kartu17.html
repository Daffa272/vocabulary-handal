1Apa itu Preposition?

Preposition adalah kata yang menghubungkan suatu kata dengan kata lain untuk menunjukkan hubungan seperti tempat, waktu, arah, atau hubungan abstrak.

2Verb + Preposition

depend on

depend on + noun / pronoun
Success depends on hard work.

❌ depend at   ❌ depend in

participate in

participate in + noun
Many students participate in the competition.

invest in

invest in + noun / V-ing
They invested money in improving the system.

apologize to / apologize for

apologize to + person · apologize for + noun/V-ing
He apologized to his teacher for being late.

concentrate on

concentrate on + noun / V-ing
Please concentrate on answering the question.

look up to

satu kesatuan — artinya "mengagumi/menghormati seseorang"
Many children look up to their parents.

5Preposition + Gerund

Jika setelah preposition terdapat kata kerja, gunakan V-ing, bukan bentuk dasar.

interested in learning · responsible for managing · successful in solving · afraid of making · concentrate on studying · capable of solving

❌ interested in learn

She is interested in learning English. ✅

6Verb + Object + Preposition

provide someone with something

The school provided students with new books.

distinguish between A and B

We must distinguish between fact and opinion.

judge someone by something

We should not judge people by their appearance.

prevent someone from doing something

The rule prevents students from using their phones.

7Between vs Among

8Of vs From

of menunjukkan hubungan, bagian, atau kepemilikan: aware of, capable of, afraid of.

from menunjukkan asal atau perbedaan: different from, come from Indonesia, information from the government.

9For vs To

for → tujuan / manfaat / karena apa: responsible for, reason for, apologize for being late.

to → penerima tindakan / kepada siapa: apologize to the teacher, provide information to students.

He apologized to his teacher for being late.

11Cara Mengerjakan Soal

  1. Identifikasi kata sebelum blank. "The students are interested ___ science." → kata kuncinya interested → ingat interested in.
  2. Jangan menerjemahkan kata per kata. "She is capable ___ solving the problem." → kenali pola capable of, bukan terjemahan literal.
  3. Perhatikan kata setelah blank. "He apologized ___ being late." → setelah blank ada V-ing (being) → pola apologize for + V-ing.
  4. Hafalkan sebagai pasangan, bukan "aware = sadar, of = dari" — tapi langsung aware of, depend on, capable of.
