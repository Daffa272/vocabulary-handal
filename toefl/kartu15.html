Adverbs & Adverbial Expressions

Kumpulan pola waktu, frekuensi, dan posisi adverb yang paling sering muncul di soal TOEFL Structure — lengkap dengan rumus cepat dan strategi menjawab.

1. waktu 2. since/for/ago 3. frekuensi 4. modal 5. just/already/yet 6. ever/never 7. still/yet 8. finally/eventually 9. during/since 10. awal kalimat 11. posisi adverb strategi
01

Adverbs of Time

keterangan waktu — kapan suatu aktivitas terjadi

adverbarticontoh
nowsekarangShe is working now.
todayhari iniHe is studying today.
tomorrowbesokThey will leave tomorrow.
yesterdaykemarinShe arrived yesterday.
recentlybaru-baru iniHe has recently moved.
nowadayszaman sekarangNowadays, people use smartphones.
eventuallyakhirnyaHe eventually found the answer.
finallyakhirnyaShe finally completed the project.
formerlydahulu / sebelumnyaThe building was formerly a school.

Perhatikan hubungan dengan tense:

Past Simple + ago — The company was established ten years ago.
Present Perfect + since — The company has operated here since 2015.
Present Perfect + for — The company has operated here for ten years.
02

Since vs For vs Ago

pola yang sangat penting dalam TOEFL

since + titik waktu

Menunjukkan awal suatu periode.

since 2015  ·  since Monday  ·  since January  ·  since the 1980s

She has lived here since 2015.

for + durasi

Menunjukkan berapa lama.

for three years  ·  for two months  ·  for ten days  ·  for several decades

She has lived here for three years.

ago

Digunakan untuk kejadian yang terjadi di masa lalu — two years ago, five days ago, several months ago.

She moved here two years ago.

rumus cepat

since → kapan mulai
for → berapa lama
ago → berapa waktu yang lalu
03

Adverbs of Frequency

seberapa sering suatu aktivitas terjadi

adverbarti
alwaysselalu
usuallybiasanya
oftensering
sometimeskadang-kadang
rarelyjarang
seldomjarang
nevertidak pernah

dengan verb biasa

Subject + adverb + verb
She usually visits the library.
They often study together.
He seldom watches television.

dengan verb BE

Subject + BE + adverb
She is usually late.
They are often busy.
04

Adverb setelah Modal

can, could, may, might, must, should, will, would

rumus

Subject + modal + adverb + V1
Students can often buy books online.
She will usually arrive early.
They should always follow the rules.
He may never understand the problem.

Jangan menggunakan "to" setelah modal:

✗ can often to buy
✗ should to study
✗ must to leave
✓ can often buy
✓ should study
✓ must leave

Setelah modal → langsung V1.

05

Just, Already, Yet

sering muncul bersama Present Perfect

just = baru saja

Subject + have/has + just + V3
She has just arrived.
The professor has just called me.

already = sudah

Subject + have/has + already + V3
She has already finished the assignment.
The researchers have already completed the experiment.

yet = belum / sudah

Biasanya digunakan dalam kalimat negatif dan pertanyaan, umumnya di akhir kalimat.

She hasn't finished yet. (negative)
Have you finished yet? (question)

perbandingan

I have already finished. = Saya sudah selesai.
I haven't finished yet. = Saya belum selesai.
Have you finished yet? = Apakah kamu sudah selesai?
06

Ever vs Never

ever = pernah

biasanya digunakan dalam pertanyaan

Have you ever visited Bali?
Has she ever seen this movie?

never = tidak pernah

She has never visited Bali.
He has never seen the movie.
07

Still vs Yet

berkaitan dengan sesuatu yang belum berubah / terjadi, posisinya berbeda

still — sebelum main verb

They still continue to collect data.
She still lives in Jakarta.

Dengan BE, diletakkan setelah BE:

She is still working.

yet — di akhir kalimat

She hasn't arrived yet.
They haven't finished the project yet.

perbandingan

She still works here. = Dia masih bekerja di sini.
She hasn't finished yet. = Dia belum selesai.
08

Finally vs Eventually vs Lastly

ketiganya bisa berarti "akhirnya", penggunaannya berbeda

Finally — tahap terakhir, sering setelah proses yang panjang.

The researchers finally completed the study.

Eventually — sesuatu akhirnya terjadi setelah beberapa waktu / proses.

He eventually discovered the answer.

Lastly — menyusun urutan atau poin terakhir.

Lastly, we need to discuss the budget.
lastly = poin terakhir
finally = tahap terakhir
eventually = akhirnya setelah proses / waktu
09

During vs Since

during + periode

during the 1980s · during the summer · during the meeting · during the experiment

Many changes occurred during the 1980s.

since + titik awal

since 1980 · since Monday · since 2015

Technology has changed significantly since 1980.

Jangan tertukar:

✗ since the 1980s (jika maksudnya "selama periode 1980-an")
✓ during the 1980s
✓ since the 1980s — sejak 1980-an sampai sekarang
10

Adverb di Awal Kalimat

beberapa adverb dapat memberikan konteks di awal kalimat

Nowadays, people communicate through smartphones.
Finally, the researchers reached a conclusion.
Recently, the university introduced a new program.
Eventually, the project was completed.
Adverb + comma + Subject + Verb

Strategi Mengerjakan Soal

saat melihat bagian kosong, tanyakan secara berurutan

  1. Apakah yang dibutuhkan adalah keterangan waktu?
    now, yesterday, tomorrow, recently, nowadays
  2. Apakah ada present perfect?
    pertimbangkan just, already, yet, ever, never, since, for
  3. Apakah kalimat menunjukkan frekuensi?
    often, usually, seldom, rarely, always
  4. Apakah ada modal?
    modal + adverb + V1
  5. Apakah ada durasi atau titik awal?
    for + durasi  /  since + titik waktu
  6. Apakah sesuatu belum terjadi?
    not + V3 + yet
