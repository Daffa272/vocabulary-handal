RANGKUMAN MATERI

TOEFL Structure & Written Expression

Empat belas konsep tata bahasa yang paling sering diuji, disusun dari subject–verb agreement hingga conditional sentence, lengkap dengan rumus, contoh, dan langkah pengerjaan.

01Subject–Verb Agreement

Subject dan verb harus sesuai dalam jumlah — singular bertemu singular, plural bertemu plural.

Singular → verb + -s/-es

  • The student studies every day.
  • The professor explains the lesson.
  • Reading regularly improves vocabulary.

Plural → verb dasar

  • The students study every day.
  • The professors explain the lessons.
  • Many people use technology.

Perhatikan subject sebenarnya

THE NUMBER OF The number of students is increasing.
Subject-nya adalah "the number" → singular.
A NUMBER OF A number of students are absent.
"A number of + plural noun" → plural verb.

02Simple Present Tense

Digunakan untuk fakta, kebiasaan, keadaan umum, dan sesuatu yang terjadi secara rutin.

Subject + V1  ·  untuk he/she/it → Subject + V1-s/-es
  • The university offers several programs.
  • Students attend classes regularly.

Kata petunjuk

usually · often · always · generally · every day · every year · regularly

03Simple Past Tense

Digunakan untuk kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu.

Subject + V2
  • The museum opened a new exhibition last year.
  • Thousands of visitors visited the museum.

Penanda waktu

yesterday · last year · last week · two days ago · in 2020 · when + kejadian masa lalu

Contoh TOEFL

When the professor arrived, the students stopped talking. Jika konteksnya jelas berada di masa lalu, gunakan V2.

04Present Perfect

Untuk kejadian yang telah terjadi, memiliki hubungan dengan keadaan sekarang, atau terjadi dalam periode waktu yang belum selesai.

Subject + has/have + V3
Auxiliary berdasarkan subject
SubjectAuxiliary
He / She / Ithas
I / You / We / Theyhave
  • The company has developed several new products.
  • People have become more aware of environmental problems.

Kata petunjuk

already · recently · just · since · for · over the past few years · in recent years

  • The students has completed the assignment.
  • The students have completed the assignment.

05Past Perfect

Digunakan ketika ada dua kejadian di masa lalu, untuk menunjukkan kejadian mana yang terjadi lebih dahulu.

Subject + had + V3

When the researchers arrived, the experiment had started.

Urutannya: experiment started → researchers arrived. Karena experiment terjadi lebih dahulu, digunakan had + V3.

Kata yang sering muncul

before · after · already · when · by the time

06Passive Voice

Digunakan ketika fokus kalimat berada pada objek yang dikenai tindakan, bukan pelakunya.

Subject + be + V3
  • The documents were reviewed by the university.
  • The historical documents are preserved carefully.
Bentuk passive berdasarkan tense
TensePassive
Simple Presentam/is/are + V3
Simple Pastwas/were + V3
Present Perfecthas/have been + V3
Futurewill be + V3
ACTIVEThe university built the library.
PASSIVEThe library was built by the university.
Jika menemukan pola "The documents ____ carefully", kemungkinan besar yang dibutuhkan adalah passive voice — terutama jika dokumen tersebut "diperiksa", "disimpan", atau "ditulis".

07Gerund

Gerund adalah V-ing yang berfungsi sebagai noun.

Reading regularly improves vocabulary — "Reading" berfungsi sebagai subject.

Gerund setelah preposition

Preposition + V-ing
  • interested in learning
  • good at speaking
  • responsible for maintaining
  • excited about visiting
  • interested in learn
  • interested in to learn
  • interested in learning

08Gerund sebagai Subject

Jika V-ing menjadi subject, biasanya dianggap singular.

  • Studying foreign languages helps students.
  • Studying foreign languages help students.

Karena subject-nya adalah aktivitas studying, maka verb-nya helps.

  • Reading regularly improves vocabulary.
  • Working with professionals gives students practical skills.

09Infinitive

Infinitive menggunakan bentuk to + V1.

to + V1
  • The professor encouraged the students to present their research.
  • Students need to study regularly.

Verb yang sering diikuti infinitive

want to · need to · decide to · encourage someone to · allow someone to · expect someone to · plan to

The teacher encouraged the students to participate.

10Conditional Sentence Type 1

Digunakan untuk kondisi yang mungkin terjadi di masa depan.

If + Simple Present, Subject + will + V1

If the weather is good, the students will visit the site.

  • If the weather will be good...
  • If the weather is good...

11Conditional Sentence Type 2

Digunakan untuk kondisi yang hipotetis, tidak nyata, atau kemungkinan kecil.

If + Simple Past, Subject + would + V1
  • If I had more time, I would study another language.
  • If the company increased its production, it would meet the demand.
Jangan tertukar — Type 1: If + Present, will + V1. Type 2: If + Past, would + V1.

12Conditional Sentence Type 3

Digunakan untuk membicarakan kondisi masa lalu yang tidak terjadi.

If + Past Perfect, would have + V3

If I had studied harder, I would have passed the examination.
Artinya: saya tidak belajar cukup keras → saya tidak lulus.

Perbandingan tiga tipe conditional
TypeIf ClauseMain Clause
Type 1Presentwill + V1
Type 2Pastwould + V1
Type 3Past Perfectwould have + V3

13Mengenali Tense dari Konteks

Jangan hanya melihat satu kata — perhatikan konteks seluruh kalimat.

  • The company ____ several new products in recent years. → Present Perfect
  • The company ____ a new product last year. → Simple Past
  • The company ____ a new product every year. → Simple Present

14Cara Mengerjakan TOEFL Structure

Lima langkah untuk mendekati setiap soal secara konsisten.

  1. Cari Subject. Tentukan siapa atau apa yang melakukan/menjadi fokus kalimat — mis. "The number of students ____ increasing" → subject sebenarnya adalah "the number".
  2. Tentukan singular atau plural. student → singular · students → plural · the number → singular · reading / studying → singular.
  3. Cari petunjuk waktu. Perhatikan kata seperti yesterday, last year, recently, since, every year, already, when, before.
  4. Tentukan bentuk verb. Present? Past? Present Perfect? Past Perfect? Passive? Gerund? Infinitive? Conditional?
  5. Periksa pola kalimat. Misalnya If + Past → would + V1; Preposition + V-ing; has/have + V3; had + V3; be + V3.
